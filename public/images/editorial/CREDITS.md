# Editorial page photography

Real, free Unsplash photographs, downloaded October 2, 2026. License for every photo below: https://unsplash.com/license (not Unsplash+).

These illustrate the market, ingredients, growing and food preparation. They are not portraits of FreshPick staff or suppliers, photographs of a specific FreshPick farm, product listing photos, or promises about the contents of a recurring plan. Product, recipe, journal article and creator records retain their own uploaded images.

Locally hosted WebP, cropped to 4:3. Width: 1200 px, except the countryside image at 1040 px. Compact browsing headers crop further to a landscape frame; full story headers use 4:3.
