# Website CMS and search strategy
Admin > Publishing > Website pages: editable main headings, introductions, SEO titles and meta descriptions for 11 public pages; 14 homepage sections can be reordered, renamed, introduced, enabled or hidden. Page drafts and live settings are separate, with optimistic version checks and explicit publishing. Layout, navigation, imagery and fixed card copy remain component-managed; entries, guides, traditions and events use the content CMS. No arbitrary HTML or scripting.
Admin > Food & Culture CMS > Discovery guides: publish Comparison, Meal ideas, Place guide and Food guide articles with a direct answer, source, aliases, key facts, related dish slugs and optional dining search term. Place guides require a location at publication. Guide pages have Article JSON-LD, canonical URLs, visible updated dates and related links. Only published records appear in sitemap, which includes actual update timestamps. Filter/search URLs are noindex, follow; do not manufacture thousands of thin local pages. The two initial guides are original editorial content based on Sri Lanka Tourism and Explore Sri Lanka, not restaurant rankings. No fabricated pricing, dietary claims or ratings.
Next: verified location hubs (Colombo first), real menu/price comparisons with dated source records, linked business entities, editorial review workflow, useful original photos and citations, multilingual human-reviewed pages. Submit sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, monitor search queries, impressions, clicks, organic discovery sessions, AI citation visibility and factual accuracy. Test 30 prompts monthly across target assistants. No guaranteed ranking or citation.
