# FreshPick: the independent Sri Lankan food discovery hub
The current merged foundation includes approved businesses, actual published products and menu items, owner-authored articles, independent editorial references, direct contact and browser-local saved items. Do not equate an editorial reference with a registered partner or independent review.

## Differentiation
The primary job is deciding what to try and why, not ordering food. Sri Lankan competitors include MealHere (menus and discovery), Yamu and Pulse (editorial), and delivery platforms PickMe and Uber Eats. International references: TasteAtlas (dish/region knowledge), The Infatuation (occasion guides) and Beli (personal lists). Original opportunity: connect dishes, venues, brands/products, editorial evidence and local experiences in a single searchable model, with freshness and source provenance. No claim of global uniqueness.

## Milestones
1. Content foundation: start Colombo/Western Province, review 30–50 dining venues, 20 independent brands, 30 real menu/product records, 10 original guides and 5 linked dish pages. Obtain photography rights, verify current prices and sources, label owner vs editorial vs sponsored material; use Sinhala/Tamil search aliases with human review. A verified owner claim and duplicate resolution workflow precedes large-scale ingestion.
2. Discovery collections: structured occasions, cuisine, town, budget and dietary tags with evidence; shareable collection pages, dish-to-venue links, product-to-maker links, editorial article links. Search by dish and place with normalized synonyms, a map/list view, and sensible empty states. Don't advertise unverified dietary safety or live availability.
3. FreshPick This Week: dated new openings, seasonal menus, pop-ups, tasting events, chef stories and artisan releases. Expire time-limited content. Businesses submit updates; editorial team moderates. Social channels distribute these original records. Add save/share/return measurement without invasive tracking.
4. My Food Map: account-synced lists, shareable itineraries, optional follows, and deliberate preference-based recommendations. No mandatory accounts for browsing. Test repeat use before launching public review feeds.
5. Booking and business analytics only after venue adoption. Native availability and request/confirmation system with capacity controls, opt-in calendar sync, transparent sponsored placements and verified enquiry reporting.

## Social setup

No placeholder accounts or inferred handles. Existing site organization sameAs automatically reflects valid configured links. Social share on articles uses the native share sheet and copy-link fallback; no automatic publishing or account linking. Platform ownership and URL availability must be verified before adding values; redeploy afterward.

## Success metrics
Start with qualified discovery sessions (at least one meaningful save, article read or outbound business contact), 7/30-day return, guide-to-profile clicks, outbound contacts, profile completeness and freshness, and businesses publishing updates monthly. Validate against actual analytics, not invented traffic. Monetization after demonstrated usefulness: labeled sponsorships, optional business insights, verified referrals and bookings. No checkout or delivery expansion.
