# Discovery taxonomy and search visibility
Dining contains restaurant/café/bakery/caterer business profiles; Food & Brands contains independent makers/brands/other food businesses and a separate tab for concrete published products (including packaged products from bakeries where available). Imported home kitchen profiles belong in dining. Existing owner-managed maker records cannot be reliably reclassified automatically; review legacy business types individually. Gifting is a secondary supplier collection, no planner or checkout. Existing /gifting URL retained. Newsletter and visible footer support email removed; contact page retained.
SEO: separate intent-specific titles and descriptions, canonical paginated listings, noindex follow for search/filter combinations, deduplicated sitemap, organization describes discovery across Sri Lanka, article and reviewed-profile WebPage markup, updated social preview. Search crawlers remain allowed by existing robots rules. No fabricated reviews, business hours or rankings. Google Search Central https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide consulted. No special AI markup or guarantee of inclusion. After deployment verify live rendered metadata, sitemap, Search Console/Bing indexing and query performance. No new migrations or production data writes.
