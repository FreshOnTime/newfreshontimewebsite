# Discovery usability release
Account selection uses charcoal and leaf-green cards. Member and business login journeys are explained separately; member saved picks remain browser-local, and businesses need approval before publishing. Business registration and contact/enquiry APIs are unchanged. 404, route and root error recovery provide useful links; unknown/unpublished routes remain noindex. Native selects retain accessible labels and keyboard focus, with consistent 48px controls. Footer Explore is split into Explore and Food & culture.
Maker-first browsing: /food-products permanently redirects to /makers while preserving recognized q, area/town, kind and page parameters; old product-scope Explore links resolve to makers; nested product/menu detail URLs remain indexable and saved links valid. Global keyword search may still surface matching individual products. The retired page's saved CMS configuration is retained for backward compatibility but hidden from the page picker. Supplier photos are actual published offering images where available; a neutral monogram is used when there is no permitted image or an image fails. Independent prospect listings are not owner-claimed and do not invent products. Business profile and individual offering cards are square-image panels with light-green actions. No data migrations. Browser regressions cover filters, legacy redirects, recovery, account cards, product image aspect and article CTA. No production changes made.
