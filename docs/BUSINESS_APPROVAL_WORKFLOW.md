# Business approval boundaries
Owner creates a login, chooses classification and submits business details. The linked account is assigned supplier role; the row starts pending/inactive and admins receive in-app notifications. A pending owner sees an explicit status page, not publishing tools. An administrator reviews evidence and explicitly confirms identity/contact before approving. Approval opens Studio but does not publish a page. Owner then saves and publishes a profile, offerings and articles. Public queries require approved, active, published profile and public handle/contact. Revoking approval hides the page and clears its published flag; reapproval does not automatically republish. Live vendors additionally need their own approval and an unexpired check-in. External editorial references and imported review records are not owner accounts. Historical uploads are legacy, not required for onboarding. No records deleted, no existing account approved in this release.
Production audit: 3 business rows, 1 pending, 2 approved, 0 published, 0 offerings, 0 enquiries, 0 live vendors, 0 imported prospects, 1 historical upload. The three rows have linked accounts but none email-verified; independent identity unverified. Static editorial references are separate. The two metalabs rows should be reviewed for duplication, not automatically merged or deleted.
