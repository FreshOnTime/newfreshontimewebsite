# FreshPick market banner

The homepage hero implements the approved mixed-produce campaign: papaya, bananas, vegetables and greens on pale stone, with the headline “Fresh food. Full of life.” in brand green and an amber “Shop the market” link to `/products`.

The background is a text-free, AI-generated illustrative asset stored at `public/images/home/fresh-market-hero.webp` and optimized to WebP. Asset provenance is recorded in the accompanying `CREDITS.md`. It makes no sourcing or supplier claims.

The heading, supporting copy and shop link remain accessible HTML. At widths below 1024px, the image sits below the copy with a crop focused on the produce; larger screens use a photographic backdrop behind the left-aligned copy. The image is preloaded and requests responsive widths through Next Image. The existing secondary shopping links and homepage journal remain in place.
